How to play

Four corners.
Four games.四個角落，
四種遊戲。

Every card carries four independent play signals. Choose the game for your table size and read only its matching corner. One pack starts Basic Play; two or more packs activate universal abilities.每張卡牌四角各有獨立遊戲資訊。按桌上人數選擇玩法，只需讀取對應角落。一包即可開始基本玩法；兩包或以上會啟動通用技能。

Card context

What the card is.卡牌代表甚麼。

Content family and material rarity are separate. Any family may appear in multiple material treatments; a Girl is not automatically rarer than a Venue, and a named benefit remains a Gift.卡牌內容類別與材質稀有度是兩套獨立系統。任何類別都可使用不同材質；人物卡不一定比場地卡稀有，具名禮遇仍屬禮遇卡。

Material hierarchy

From matte
to mythic.由霧面，
至傳奇。

Every tier uses approximately 350gsm premium stock at 63 × 88 mm. Surface treatment creates the chase while the card remains sleeve, binder and top-loader compatible.所有級別均採用約 350gsm 高級紙材，尺寸為 63 × 88 毫米。不同表面工藝營造收藏價值，並兼容標準卡套、卡簿與硬卡夾。

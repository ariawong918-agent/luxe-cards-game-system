Universal abilities

One ability language works across all four games. An ability activates only when that card is committed.

◉

Peek

BEFORE · 15%

Randomly view one card from one chosen opponent, then lock the Peek card as your play.

◇

Shield

AFTER · 25%

Cancel your normal one-sip loss for this round. It can also block a transferred sip.

↝

Transfer

AFTER · 60%

Move your one sip to another eligible player. It never adds a sip and cannot chain.

Reward flow

Rewards come from opening the card, never from winning the drinking game. The pack buyer always owns the reward.

01

Open the sealed pack and reveal the exact printed reward.

02

Staff enters the unique card serial in the shared POS or form.

03

System confirms valid-unused or already redeemed.

04

Fulfil immediately before the game starts.

05

Customer keeps the redeemed card; it remains playable.
